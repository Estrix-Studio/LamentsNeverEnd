LoopShift — dependency and content notices

LoopShift source is supplied under the included LICENSE file.
The included demo texture and room visuals are original geometric content produced by the repository's sample-generation tooling.

No third-party fonts, audio, models, artwork or runtime libraries are bundled with this package.

External dependencies:
- Unity Engine, including its built-in IMGUI and 2D physics modules, is provided by Unity under its own license.
- The sample shader includes UnityCG.cginc from the installed Unity Editor. That engine include is not redistributed here.
- Optional package tests reference Unity Test Framework/NUnit supplied through Unity's package system. Their binaries and source are not bundled.
- Universal Render Pipeline is installed separately when used by the consumer project. LoopShift does not bundle URP.

The host game's art, dialogue, fonts and third-party packages are excluded from LoopShift distribution.
This notice describes the inspected package content; it does not replace the applicable Unity or Asset Store agreements.
